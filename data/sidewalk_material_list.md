For Ward 6 sidewalk restoration or similar temporary concrete sidewalk work.

## 5-line core list (adjust quantities to actual SOW)

2. **#4 rebar (1/2")** — 200 linear feet (or wire mesh equivalent)
3. **Form lumber (2x6 or 2x8) + stakes** — 200 linear feet forms + 50 stakes
4. **Crushed stone / gravel base (3/4")** — 5 cubic yards
5. **Expansion joint material + concrete sealant** — 50 linear feet joint + 2 tubes sealant
